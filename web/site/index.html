DRIFT

Dream firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Drift turns the FM-1 into a small, simple instrument for dreamy, ambient, meditative music. Press PLAY and a gentle chord progression starts and keeps going by itself. Add and remove layers with the white keys, move the harmony with the black keys, and shape the sound live with four knobs. Every note fits, so it always sounds good. No menus, no drums.

I'm not a music expert, and Drift isn't meant to be a full synthesizer. It deliberately limits what the FM-1 can do, so all that's left is playing melodic, drifting sounds. I use it for live meditation, together with my singing bowls.

The browser version runs the same code as the instrument, with sound. Chrome or Edge recommended.

HOW TO PLAY

PLAYStarts and stops the music. Everything begins here.
KNOB 1–4Space, Echo, Glow, Motion: reverb, delay, brightness, and how busy it is. The main way to play live.
White keysLayers on and off, lit while playing: pad, bass, arpeggio, sparkle, melody, voice, shimmer.
Black keysChords to move to. Press to go there, hold to stay, let go and the progression carries on.
PRESETSThe world: sounds, key, tempo and room.
ALGORITHMThe behaviour: drift, ripple, swell, chime or pulse.
SELECTThe progression: drone, home, float, longing, rise or wander.
OCT − / +Everything an octave down or up.
FX … GLOPunch-in effects while held, like a DJ, on the beat: echoes, stutter, freeze, shimmer, underwater, wash.

INSTALLING

Connect the FM-1 by USB, open the installer in Chrome or Edge and press Install. Nothing else is needed. The installer can also take you back to M-VAVE's official firmware (V15) at any time.

Installing custom firmware is at your own risk. Drift is young and has had little testing on real devices. Keep M-VAVE's official V15 firmware file ready before you install.
